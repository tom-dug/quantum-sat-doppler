# Eccentricity e = shape of the trajectory

![bg left:60% 100%](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/00/Ellipse_Parameter_Diagram.png)


- ellipse e<1
- perfect circle e=0
- parabola e=1
- hyperbola e>1

---

# Semi-major axis a : half the distance between the apoapsis and periapsis (long axis of the ellipse)

![bg left:60% 100%](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/00/Ellipse_Parameter_Diagram.png)

- elliptical/circle orbits a>0
- parabolic trajectories a undefined
- hyperbolic trajectories a<0

---

# Semi-minor axis b : half the short axis through the geometric center of the ellipse

![bg left:60% 100%](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/00/Ellipse_Parameter_Diagram.png)

- same than a

---

# Semi-parameter p : half the width of the orbit perpendicular to the periapsis direction, crossing the primary focus (the orbital radius r for a true anomaly of ±90° ).

- always defined and >0

![bg left:60% 100%](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/00/Ellipse_Parameter_Diagram.png)

---

# Apoapsis ra (sometimes Q) : the farthest point in the orbit from the central body (at a true anomaly of π radians, or  180° ). 

![bg left:60% 100%](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/00/Ellipse_Parameter_Diagram.png)

- parabolic/hyperbolic trajectories ra undefined (or infinity)

---

# Periapsis rp (sometimes q): the closest point in the orbit from the central body (at a true anomaly of 0)

![bg left:60% 100%](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/00/Ellipse_Parameter_Diagram.png)

- defined for all orbit types
